OFFLINE SECURITY APPLICATION

Privacy Policy for SentryX

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

SentryX (we, our, or the App) is an offline, on-device anti-theft security and motion detection alarm utility. We are firmly committed to user privacy and total data sovereignty.

1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Operation

SentryX does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize any personal, financial, biometric, sensor, or usage data. The application operates entirely offline on your device and does not require nor request access to the Internet (android.permission.INTERNET is completely absent from our application).

2. Hardware Sensors & Device Usage

When you explicitly arm the device shield, the app monitors device motion and proximity sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, and proximity sensor) purely in real-time RAM to detect unauthorized physical movement, pocket picking, or lifting. Sensor readings:

  • Are processed strictly on-device in real-time.
  • Are never recorded, logged to persistent storage, or transmitted off your phone.
  • Cease immediate evaluation the moment the shield is disarmed or authenticated.

3. Biometric & Authentication Data

When disarming the alarm, SentryX uses Android's native BiometricPrompt and Device Credential API (Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or PIN/Pattern).

  • Biometric data is handled entirely by the secure Android operating system hardware (TEE / StrongBox).
  • SentryX never has access to, sees, or stores your biometric templates or passwords. It only receives a cryptographic success or failure callback from the OS.

4. Foreground Service & Notifications

SentryX runs a foreground service with a persistent notification (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) solely to maintain active sensor observation while your phone's screen is turned off or in standby. This service is 100% user-initiated and can be terminated at any moment directly by disarming the shield or tapping the notification.

5. Third-Party Libraries and Advertisements

SentryX does not integrate any third-party analytics (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), tracking SDKs, or advertising networks. Your device experience is completely private and unmonitored.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our security practices, you may reach out directly via our developer contact email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
